I am a sophomore majoring in Computer Science at the IIIS, Tsinghua University. Currently, I am an intern at Mars Lab, supervised by Professor Hang Zhao. My research interests lie in computer vision and deep generative models. I am particularly fascinated by simple yet effective model designs.
Before entering college, I competed in the Mathematics Olympiad during high school and won a gold medal in the 38th CMO. Afterward, I spent one preparatory year at IIIS, Tsinghua University, where I built a solid foundation in the algorithms behind deep learning and computer science.
Although I am still early in my research journey, I am eager to explore opportunities in both academia and industry. I also enjoy connecting with people who share similar interests. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to connect!
